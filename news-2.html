AI-Powered Surveillance & Smart Access Control for Modern Facilities

Discover how integrating AI perimeter analytics, automated biometric access gates, and 24/7 intelligent CCTV surveillance shields corporate campuses and public infrastructure from security threats.

Physical security has evolved far beyond passive video recording. Today's commercial office parks, financial institutions, and government facilities require active, real-time threat detection capable of instantly distinguishing authorized personnel from unauthorized breaches.

The Shift to Intelligent Surveillance

Traditional CCTV required constant manual monitoring by security officers, leading to fatigue and missed alerts. At Obmask Solutions, our security solutions incorporate computer vision AI analytics directly at the camera node level. This enables automated license plate recognition (ALPR), facial verification, heat map crowd monitoring, and instant perimeter fence intrusion alerts sent directly to command centers.

"Smart security is about prevention, not just investigation. Real-time video analytics convert video feeds into actionable intelligence within milliseconds."

Integrated Access Control Systems

Comprehensive facility defense combines smart CCTV with automated access control hardware:

  • Biometric & RFID Turnstiles: Touchless entry for employees via facial recognition or mobile NFC credentials.
  • Automated Vehicle Boom Barriers: Integrated ALPR opens gates automatically for registered staff vehicles.
  • Centralized Control Dashboard: Manage permissions, guest passes, and audit trails from a single cloud panel.

Professional Deployment in Abuja & Regional Hubs

Obmask Solutions handles the complete lifecycle—from initial vulnerability assessment and cable conduit laying to camera placement optimization and security team training. Protect your assets with industry-tested, resilient security hardware built for 24/7 reliability.
